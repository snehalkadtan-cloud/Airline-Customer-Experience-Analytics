# ✈️ Airline Customer Experience Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes airline customer experience data using **Tableau** to understand passenger satisfaction and identify the key service factors influencing the overall travel experience.

The analysis focuses on customer satisfaction across travel classes, service quality, flight distance, and departure delays to identify areas where airlines can improve passenger experience.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

* Analyze overall passenger satisfaction.
* Compare satisfaction across different travel classes.
* Evaluate airline service quality across different service categories.
* Understand the relationship between departure delays and passenger experience.
* Analyze the relationship between flight distance and satisfaction.
* Identify opportunities to improve overall customer satisfaction.

## Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** — Data preparation and cleaning
* **Tableau Public** — Data analysis, visualization, and interactive dashboard development

## Dashboard Analysis

The Tableau dashboard includes:

* Overall passenger satisfaction KPIs
* Satisfaction by travel class
* Service rating comparison
* Service quality by category
* Departure delay analysis
* Flight distance vs. passenger satisfaction
* Interactive filters for exploring customer segments

## Key Insights

* Passenger satisfaction varies across different travel classes.
* Service-related factors play an important role in determining overall customer satisfaction.
* Certain service categories receive stronger ratings than others, highlighting specific areas requiring improvement.
* Departure delays can negatively affect the overall passenger experience.
* Customer satisfaction patterns vary across different flight distances and passenger groups.

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize improvements in service categories receiving comparatively lower customer ratings.
2. Analyze the causes of departure delays and improve operational processes to reduce passenger inconvenience.
3. Develop targeted customer-experience strategies for different travel classes rather than applying the same service approach to every passenger.
4. Continuously monitor customer satisfaction KPIs to identify emerging service-quality issues.
5. Use passenger feedback and satisfaction data to prioritize service improvements with the greatest potential impact.
